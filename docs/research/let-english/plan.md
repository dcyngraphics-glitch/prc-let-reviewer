# Deep research plan — PRC LET reviewer for English majors

Date: 2026-11-22. Tier: Standard (5 researchers, up to 1 gap round, 1–2 verifiers).

## Scope block
- **Question:** What is the authoritative subject-by-subject coverage and exam mechanics of the Philippine PRC Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET), with emphasis on the English major, that should drive a mobile HTML reviewer with a timer and a limited per-question clue button?
- **Decision it supports:** question-bank structure, exam-mode rules (items per subject, time limit), and clue budget design (5–10 clues per session).
- **Audience:** Cyron (builder) and LET English-major examinees.
- **Geography / time frame:** Philippines; current rules 2024–2026; flag older sources.
- **Deliverable:** cited report `report.md`, topic outline for question generation, and verified exam rules.
- **User premise to test, not assume:** "PRC is 150 items each subject per hour." Check official item count AND official time per subject; do not repeat the claim unless sourced.
- **Clue premise:** every question must have a ready clue; 5–10 clue uses per exam session. Research evidence on hints/cues in practice tests where available.

## Working hypotheses
1. LET uses 150 items per subject, but the time per subject is likely 3 hours, not 1 hour — verify.
2. English-major coverage splits into language/linguistics, literature, composition, and pedagogy.
3. Gen Ed and Professional Education are separate areas from the English major concentration.

## Strongest opposing view to find evidence for
Some reviewer sites describe the exam as 150 items total per day or a different subject count/time allocation; if found, report it with source quality comparison.
