# LET English reviewer — research report

Date: 2026-11-22. Question: what exam rules and topic coverage should a phone-first LET reviewer for English majors enforce?

**Bottom line (confidence: High).** 150 items per subject is correct for Gen Ed, Prof Ed and the English specialization (Math is 120), but "per hour" is wrong: PRC's 2025 and 2026 programs schedule Gen Ed 2 h, Prof Ed 3 h and Specialization 3.5 h. The English majorship's current blueprint is the 2025-amended Enhanced TOS — 150 items over 22 areas, weighted 25% theory / 30% literature / 45% methodology. Two independent verifiers confirmed the timing, item counts, passing rule and all three TOS tables against the cited PRC documents. Confidence is lowered only for the legacy-vs-current Gen Ed conflict, which no source reconciles.

## Key findings
1. **Timing and rules [1][2][3][6][7].** Secondary LET: Gen Ed 150/120 min, Prof Ed 150/180 min, English 150/210 min; weights 20/40/40. Pass = average >=75% with no part below 50% (1996 resolution, still restated in 2021 and 2026 reporting). Confidence High (verified).
2. **English TOS [1][4].** Section A theoretical foundations 25% (linguistics 15, language/culture/society 3, structure of English 17, language programs/policies 3); B literature 30% (children/adolescent 12, mythology/folklore 7, survey of selected countries 20, contemporary/popular/emergent 6); C methodology 45% (macroskills 15, literature teaching 15, materials 3, speech/theatre 3, research 4, criticism 3, stylistics 3, campus journalism 3, technical writing 3, technology 3, translation/editing 3, ESP 3, remedial 3, creative writing 3). Confidence High (verified).
3. **Gen Ed [1][5][8].** Current TOS is 10 areas x 15 items: Purposive Communication, Malayuning Komunikasyon, Philippine History and Society, Rizal, Contemporary World, Art Appreciation, Science and Technology, Mathematics, Ethics, Understanding the Self. Older program bulletins (still republished in 2026) list 5 areas: English, Filipino, Math, Science, Social Sciences. Support both category shapes; TOS is primary. Confidence High for both lists existing; unresolved which the item writer intended.
4. **Prof Ed [1][4][9].** Five areas: Teaching Profession 23, Curriculum/Methods/EdTech 45, Child & Adolescent 30, Assessment 22, Field Study/Internship 30. Filipino is Gen Ed for all plus a separate Filipino-major specialization — out of scope for English majors. Confidence High (verified).
5. **Items and clues [10][11][12].** No official released LET items with keys were found. A free 1,500-item third-party English bank had answer keys, but also 22 N/P keys, 14 placeholders and 38 duplicate stems — import selectively. Item-writing guidance: 4 parallel options, positive stems, no "all of the above", balanced keys, rationale per option. Evidence on hints: strong cues speed initial recall but worsen final retention; fading cues help weak learners. A 5–10 use cap is a product default, not a research finding. Confidence Medium (secondary summaries; primary hint paper blocked).

## Where sources disagree
- 5-area vs 10-area Gen Ed: the dated TOS is the exam blueprint; the 5-area list is still published as current. No source reconciles them — treat as alias mapping, not a settled contradiction.
- "Composition" and "phonetics/IPA" are common reviewer labels but not official TOS areas; phonology is official, composition lives under writing areas and legacy Gen Ed.
- Several commercial sites give 2 h for every subject or a 2-day exam; these are low-quality and internally inconsistent, outweighed by the PRC programs.

## Unknowns and limits
- CHED CMO 82/75 PDFs were blocked; the TOS is cited directly, so this does not change exam rules.
- Annex A-1 (new ECEd/SNed/split-TLE item counts) not found; irrelevant to English majors.
- No 2024–2026 primary document restates the passing rule; 1996 + 2021 primary sources and 2026 secondary reporting agree.
- The owner's Sir Jay PDF was encrypted; it was unlocked with the owner's password on 2026-11-22 and extracted to `docs/gen-ed-book.txt` (491 pages, 12,000+ Q&A with rationales).
